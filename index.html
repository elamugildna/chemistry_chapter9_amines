3. Key Reaction Mechanisms & NCERT Exceptions

  • Gabriel Phthalimide Synthesis: Used for preparing pure primary aliphatic amines. Aromatic primary amines cannot be prepared because aryl halides do not undergo nucleophilic substitution with phthalimide anion.
  • Hoffmann Bromamide Degradation: Primary amide + Br2 + 4NaOH → Primary amine with one less carbon atom + Na2CO3 + 2NaBr + 2H2O.
  • Aniline Nitration: Direct nitration with conc. HNO3/H2SO4 gives 51% para, 47% meta, and 2% ortho because strongly acidic medium protonates aniline into meta-directing anilinium ion (-NH3+). Controlled nitration requires prior protection by acetylation to acetanilide.
  • Friedel-Crafts Reaction: Aniline fails to undergo Friedel-Crafts alkylation or acylation because the basic -NH2 group forms an adduct salt with Lewis acid catalyst AlCl3.
  • Sulphanilic Acid Zwitterion: Heating aniline with conc. H2SO4 at 453–473 K forms sulphanilic acid, which exists predominantly as the dipolar Zwitter ion (+NH3-C6H4-SO3-).
